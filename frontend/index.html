What to do with it

Three plays that follow directly from the analysis above.

Move month-to-month customers onto longer terms

43% of month-to-month customers churn, against 11% on one-year and 3% on two-year contracts. Offer a discount or perk for committing.

Protect the first months

Tenure has the strongest negative link to churn (−0.35). Give new customers free premium service for the first three months and a named onboarding contact.

Fix support and offer a cheaper plan

Customers without online security or tech support churn far more. Give at-risk customers priority support, and a lighter package with fewer services if the bill is the problem.
